03 · Projects

Things I’ve built

Each one began with a real problem.

Triage Assist

Clarity for frontline health workers

When an ASHA worker meets a patient, minutes and judgement matter. Triage Assist runs four cooperating AI agents that read the symptoms, retrieve trusted medical guidance and weigh the urgency, then return one simple answer: red, yellow or green. Retrieval-augmented generation over ChromaDB keeps every verdict grounded, with Llama 3.1 on Groq doing the reasoning.

LangGraphRAGLlama 3.1GroqStreamlit

ScamShield

A scam detector that speaks your language

Scammers don’t stick to one language, so neither does ScamShield. Type a message or simply speak it: Whisper Large V3 transcribes, Groq models translate and classify, and a BM25 search engine I coded from scratch checks it against known scam patterns. You get a clear verdict in the language you understand.

WhisperGroq LLMBM25 from scratchFastAPIMultilingual

Smart Rescue Beacon

Finding survivors when every minute counts

After a collapse, there is no phone signal and no time. This ESP32 device listens for sound, vibration, gas, temperature and motion, fuses them into a survivor-probability score and flags GREEN, YELLOW or RED. Alerts travel over long-range LoRa radio to a Java/JavaFX dashboard with SQLite logging, so rescuers know where to dig first. Edge intelligence, from hardware to dashboard.

ESP32LoRaSensor fusionEdge computingJavaFX

AI Traffic Violation Detection

Computer vision that keeps roads honest

Watching hours of traffic footage by hand doesn’t scale. This computer-vision system looks at the road the way an officer would, spots vehicles breaking the rules and marks them with evidence in the frame. Faster enforcement, fewer misses, and a fairer road for everyone.

Computer VisionPythonDeep Learning

Churn Radar

Know who’s leaving, and exactly why

Most churn models give you a number and a shrug. Churn Radar, built in SQL on Exasol, scores every customer and shows the reasons behind each score, so a team knows what to fix before the customer walks away.
